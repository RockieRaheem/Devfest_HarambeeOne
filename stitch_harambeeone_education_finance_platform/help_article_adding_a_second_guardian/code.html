How to add a second guardian

Adding a second guardian allows another trusted adult to securely view your child's academic progress, receive important school notices, and share responsibilities such as fee payments. Both guardians will have equal access to the portal's core features.

checklist What you'll need

Before starting, ensure you have the following information for the new guardian:

  • check_circle Full Legal Name
  • check_circle Relationship to Child
  • check_circle National ID Number
  • check_circle Active Phone Number

verified_user Guardian Permissions

Choose the level of access for the new guardian:

  • check_circle

    Full Access (Co-Guardian)

    Can view all records, receive all notices, and make payments.

  • check_circle

    View Only

    Can view academic progress and notices but cannot initiate financial transactions.

Step-by-step Instructions

1

Navigate to the Children section

Tap on the 'Children' tab located in the main navigation menu.

2

Select the specific profile

Tap on the child's profile to which you wish to add a guardian.

3

Initiate addition

Scroll down to the 'Guardians' section and tap the 'Add Guardian' button.

4

Provide details

Enter the required details (gathered in the previous step) and securely upload a clear copy of their national ID.

5

Submit for verification

Review the information and tap 'Submit'. The request will be sent to administration for review.

Updating Guardian Information

If you need to update a guardian's contact details or legal name, follow these steps:

1

Go to the Child Profile

Select the child's profile and scroll to the Guardians section.

2

Select Edit

Tap the pencil icon next to the name of the guardian you wish to update.

3

Submit Changes

Make the necessary edits and tap Save. Updates to legal names or national ID numbers may require administrative re-verification and typically take 24-48 hours to process.

Removing a Guardian

If you need to revoke access for an existing guardian, follow these steps:

1

Navigate to the child's profile

Go to the 'Children' section and select the specific child.

2

Locate the guardian

Scroll to the 'Guardians' list and tap the 'Edit' icon next to the guardian's name.

3

Confirm removal

Select 'Remove Guardian' and confirm the action.

info

Removing a guardian is effective immediately and they will lose all access to the child's information.

info

Important Note

To ensure the security of student data, all new guardian additions require administrative approval. Verification typically takes 24-48 hours during standard business days.
