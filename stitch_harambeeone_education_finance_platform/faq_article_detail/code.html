How do I pay tuition online?

Updated 2 weeks ago

1

Log in to your portal and select 'Payments' from the bottom menu.

2

Choose the student you wish to pay for.

3

Select the invoice or enter a custom amount.

4

Choose your payment method (Mobile Money or Bank Transfer) and confirm.
