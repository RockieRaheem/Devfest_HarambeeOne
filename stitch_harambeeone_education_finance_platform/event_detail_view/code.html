info Event Details

The Annual Science Fair is the highlight of our STEM calendar. Students from all year groups have been working diligently on projects ranging from renewable energy models to biological surveys of the local ecosystem.

Parents and guardians are warmly invited to attend and interact with the students. Judging will take place in the morning, and the hall will be open to the public from 11:00 AM onwards. Awards will be presented at 2:30 PM.
