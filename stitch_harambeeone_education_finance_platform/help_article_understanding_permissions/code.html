Understanding permissions

Permissions control what information guardians can see and what actions they can take within the HarambeeOne Parent Portal. Assigning the correct permission level ensures that sensitive financial data is protected while maintaining transparency for all caregivers.

admin_panel_settings

Full Access

Co-Guardian

This level provides unrestricted access to the student's profile. Full Access guardians are typically primary caregivers responsible for financial commitments.

  • check_circle View all student academic data and records
  • check_circle Receive all school notices and emergency alerts
  • check_circle Initiate financial transactions and manage payments
visibility

View Only

Restricted

Designed for extended family members or secondary caregivers who need to stay informed about academic progress but are not responsible for fees.

  • check_circle View academic reports and attendance
  • check_circle Receive school notices
  • cancel Cannot initiate financial transactions

Primary vs. Secondary Guardian

person

Primary Guardian

The main point of contact, legally responsible for all financial obligations, tuition fees, and child data management. They serve as the primary billing contact for all school transactions.

group

Secondary Guardian

A supporting role for additional caregivers. They can have either Full or View Only access but do not hold primary financial responsibility. They can view the ledger but are not responsible for fee settlements unless designated as a co-payer.

info

Note: Only the Primary Guardian can authorize recurring payment schedules or change billing methods for mandatory school fees.

How to change permissions

Primary Guardians can update the permission levels of secondary caregivers through the guardian management settings.

  1. Go to the Profile tab in the main navigation.
  2. Select Guardian Management.
  3. Tap the guardian's name whose permissions you wish to change.
  4. Select Edit Permissions and choose between 'Full Access' or 'View Only'.
  5. Tap Save Changes to apply.
