## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the discipline of institutional endowment accounting and collegiate trust administration. Designed for bursars, endowment managers, scholarship trustees, and institutional finance directors, the UI communicates absolute fiscal clarity, fiduciary stewardship, and timeless academic prestige. 

The aesthetic is grounded in high-end print editorial design and fine ledger craftsmanship. It completely rejects digital gimmicks—no luminous gradients, no decorative frosted glass, and no tinted background washes. The interface reads like crisp, heavy-weight rag paper: pristine white backdrops, razor-sharp hairline borders, strict geometric alignment, and rich charcoal-to-black ink typography. Surfaces convey authority through spatial economy, uncompromising contrast, and tabular precision.

## Colors

The color palette is strictly restrained, structured around pure paper tones and deep archival inks:

- **Base Surfaces:** Pure White (`#ffffff`) serves as the core canvas and elevated surface. Auxiliary working sheets, data trays, and alternating row structures use true neutral paper off-whites (`#fcfcfc`, `#f8f9fa`). Warm cream, sepia, beige, and synthetic cool-blue tinted fills are strictly forbidden.
- **Inks & Typography:** Primary typographic marks and data values use Deep Slate Black (`#0f172a`) and Core Carbon (`#111827`), providing maximum contrast and print-grade legibility. Secondary labels, ledger headers, and metadata utilize Muted Ledger Slate (`#64748b`). Hairline boundaries and cell separators strictly use Neutral Slate Hairlines (`#e2e4e8` and `#e5e7eb`).
- **Primary Ink Accent:** Deep Navy Ink (`#0f2b48`) is deployed sparingly. It is reserved strictly for primary execution triggers, certified status seals, active financial balance highlights, and official ledger signature blocks.
- **Auditing Accents:** Functional accounting statuses rely on desaturated ink tones: Audited Clear/Surplus (`#047857`), Discrepancy/Debit (`#b91c1c`), and In-Review/Pending Allocation (`#b45309`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial dignity of **Source Serif 4** with the engineered precision of **Hanken Grotesk**:

- **Headlines & Statements:** Source Serif 4 conveys scholarly gravitas, institutional permanence, and verified legitimacy. It is applied to summary page titles, total balance declarations, endowment names, and section milestones.
- **Interface & Data Grid:** Hanken Grotesk functions as the utilitarian instrument for ledger rows, metric tags, analytical charts, form inputs, and column descriptors.
- **Tabular Figures:** All financial quantities, fiscal transaction IDs, account balances, and date entries must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "zero" 1` to ensure strict vertical tabular column alignment across decimal points.
- **Ledger Headers:** Metadata labels use uppercase `label-sm` with widened letter-spacing (`0.06em`), referencing physical double-entry accounting journals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors a formal audited ledger, emphasizing architectural structural lines and absolute predictability:

- **Grid Discipline:** Desktop views employ a 12-column grid within a maximum layout container of `1440px`. Data tables span 8 to 12 columns with unyielding horizontal alignment across adjacent cards. Mobile views collapse to a single continuous column structure with compact padding.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Based on an uncompromising 4px/8px modular rhythm. Data density takes precedence over excessive negative space: table cell vertical padding is compressed (`space-sm`), while administrative summary headers receive structured breathing room (`space-xl`).
- **Hairline Containment:** Visual hierarchy is governed by vertical and horizontal containment borders rather than expansive white voids, allowing complex educational financial records to be scanned without loss of context.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system discards heavy dropshadows, blurred ambient glows, and floating layers. Depth is constructed purely through **flat hairline stratification and paper layering**:

- **Level 0 (App Canvas):** Pure background paper (`#f8f9fa`).
- **Level 1 (Work Surface / Table Deck):** Pure white sheets (`#ffffff`) bordered with a crisp `1px solid #e2e4e8` perimeter line. No drop shadow is applied; division is established strictly by border geometry and surface contrast.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus / Selected Rows):** Tinted to paper white (`#fcfcfc`) with a `1px solid #0f172a` primary bounding border.
- **Overlays, Popovers, & Certified Menus:** Pure white cards framed by a razor hairline (`1px solid #e2e4e8`) accompanied by a subtle, compressed non-diffuse print shadow (`0 2px 4px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`), replicating an index slip resting flat upon a ledger.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. Border radii are minimal and disciplined, referencing archival bookbinding and microfiche cards rather than modern consumer apps:

- **Buttons & Action Controls:** Fixed at `0.25rem` (4px). Rounded pills and circular buttons are prohibited.
- **Ledger Cards & Data Modules:** `0.25rem` (4px) to retain structural alignment with vertical grid guidelines.
- **Status Badges & Verification Seals:** `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, reinforcing their role as official physical stamps.
- **Input Fields & Selectors:** `0.25rem` (4px) with crisp interior bounds.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Commit / Issue / Approve):** Solid Deep Navy Ink (`#0f2b48`) fill with Pure White text. Hover transitions to `#163b61`. No shadow. Focus ring uses a crisp `2px offset` hairline in `#0f2b48`.
- **Secondary (Ledger Action / Filter):** Pure White (`#ffffff`) background, `1px solid #e2e4e8` border, text `#0f172a`. Hover state switches surface to `#f8f9fa` with border darkening to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Tertiary / Destructive:** Muted Slate (`#64748b`) or Crimson Ink (`#b91c1c`) text with zero background; text underlines strictly on focus or active hover states.

### Ledger Data Tables (Core Component)
- **Header Row:** Background `#f8f9fa`, border bottom `2px solid #0f172a`, text `label-sm` in `#64748b`, strictly left-aligned for accounts, right-aligned for all financial figures.
- **Data Rows:** Background `#ffffff`, border bottom `1px solid #e5e7eb`. Height locked to 40px for high-density review. Monospaced numeric rendering (`tabular-num`) for amounts, dates, and account codes.
- **Summary Row:** Border top `2px solid #0f172a`, border bottom `1px double #0f172a`, font weight bold, capturing classical double-underlining conventions of certified balance sheets.

### Verification Chips & Seals
- **Verified / Disbursed Stamp:** `#ffffff` surface, `1px solid #047857`, text `#047857`, prefixed by a small solid checkmark glyph.
- **Pending Allocation:** `#ffffff` surface, `1px solid #b45309`, text `#b45309`.
- **Flagged Audit:** `#ffffff` surface, `1px solid #b91c1c`, text `#b91c1c`.

### Forms & Input Fields
- Background `#ffffff`, `1px solid #e2e4e8`, corner radius `0.25rem`, internal padding `0.5rem 0.75rem`. Text in `#0f172a`.
- Active focus invokes a strict `1px solid #0f2b48` border with no diffuse outer glow.
- Prefixes (currency symbols `$`, institutional codes) sit within an integrated right-bordered compartment filled with `#f8f9fa`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square (`4px` radius for checkboxes, standard geometric circle for radios), `1px solid #cbd5e1`, background `#ffffff`. 
- Selected state fills with `#0f2b48` bearing an unaliased white geometric check or core dot.

### Financial Summary Cards
- Pure White (`#ffffff`) container with a uniform `1px solid #e2e4e8` border.
- Header features a subdued uppercase category label (`label-sm` in `#64748b`).
- Primary metric set in Source Serif 4 (`headline-lg` in `#0f172a`).
- Secondary delta or balance trend sits directly beneath in tabular sans, bordered by an ultra-fine internal horizontal divider (`#f1f5f9`).
